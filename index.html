    Controls

    The view orbits a focus point. The scale slider and the mouse wheel change the reference distance, the camera's distance to that focus, from 10⁷ m (Earth close-up) to 10²⁷ m. Every zoom and warp is interpolated in log₁₀(distance), so the whole range is continuous.

    Reading the screen

    The telemetry panel reports the current reference distance in four units and what the cosmological model says about a point at that comoving distance. The selected object has its own measured and calculated values. Badges mark where each number comes from: OBS observational reference data, MODEL calculated from the stated flat ΛCDM model, PROC procedural or schematic visualization.

    Planets and other bodies are drawn larger than life when zoomed out so they stay visible; Data & assumptions explains every compression and approximation.
